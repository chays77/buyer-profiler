## How the Profiler Uses This

When `user.md` is present, every close strategy output will include:

**Seller-Buyer Dynamic** — a section that names:
1. The natural rapport or friction between your type and the buyer's type
2. Your specific blind spots that are most likely to cost you *this* deal
3. One or two compensations — concrete adjustments to your natural style for this specific buyer

Example output when a Relator (Water) is selling to a Commander (Fire):

> **Seller-Buyer Dynamic**
> You lead with relationship. They lead with results. You'll build rapport fast — but you'll also tend to avoid the uncomfortable budget and next-step conversations. That's exactly where this deal dies. Force the Sandler frame even when it feels pushy. For a Commander, directness is respect, not aggression.
