# Cognitive Functions — Reference

This file is the diagnostic engine behind buyer typing. The 16 types in `buyer-types.md` are surface labels. The cognitive functions are how those types actually decide.

**When you load this file:** when you need to sharpen a type read, explain *why* a buyer is behaving the way they are, or when two types look similar on the surface and you need to tell them apart.

**Translation rule:** cognitive functions are diagnostic vocabulary. The seller never has to learn them. In output, name the stack alongside a plain-English translation. The seller reads the translation; the stack is there for the buyer who wants to go deeper or for cross-reference.

---

## The Eight Functions

Every person uses all eight, but two dominate decision-making. The dominant function is the lens you can't turn off. The auxiliary is the support tool. The third (tertiary) and fourth (inferior) are weaker — the inferior often shows up under stress as a blind spot.

### Judging Functions (how they decide)

**Te — Extraverted Thinking** — *Efficiency, metrics, external logic.* Wants the most direct path to a measurable outcome. Asks "Does this work? What's the ROI?" Sees the world as a system to optimize.

**Ti — Introverted Thinking** — *Internal precision, frameworks, principle-checking.* Wants the underlying logic to be airtight before acting. Asks "Is this internally consistent? What are the assumptions?" Will pressure-test you to find the gap.

**Fe — Extraverted Feeling** — *Group harmony, others' needs, social atmosphere.* Reads the room and adjusts. Asks "Is everyone okay with this? Who's not aligned?" Will not push a decision the team isn't comfortable with.

**Fi — Introverted Feeling** — *Personal values, authenticity, internal compass.* Decides from a private set of values. Asks "Does this align with what I believe? Does this feel right?" Won't be sold on something that violates their values, no matter the ROI.

### Perceiving Functions (how they take in information)

**Ne — Extraverted Intuition** — *Possibilities, connections, what could be.* Sees five paths where others see one. Asks "What if? What else? Where could this go?" Energized by ideas, fatigued by detail.

**Ni — Introverted Intuition** — *Insight, pattern, where this is heading.* Sees the underlying trajectory. Asks "Where does this lead? What's the long-term implication?" Holds a strong vision and tests reality against it.

**Se — Extraverted Sensing** — *The present, the tangible, what's in front of them right now.* Acts on what's real and immediate. Asks "What can we do right now? What does this actually look like?" Trusts experience over theory.

**Si — Introverted Sensing** — *Proven process, past data, what has worked.* Anchors in what's been tested. Asks "How has this worked before? What's the established way?" Trusts track record over novelty.

## The Sixteen Stacks

Each type has four functions in a fixed order: **Dominant — Auxiliary — Tertiary — Inferior**.

The dominant runs the show. The auxiliary supports. The tertiary is a developmental gear. The inferior is the blind spot — under pressure, it either disappears or shows up clumsily.

### Decisive / Fast-Moving

**Commander (ENTJ) — Te-Ni-Se-Fi**
Leads with metrics + measurable outcome (Te). Backed by a vision of where this leads (Ni). Inferior Fi means they're slow to recognize when their own values are being violated — they'll override their gut for the spreadsheet, which is why a values-pitch lands late but powerfully when it does.
**How they decide:** efficiency first, vision underneath, gut last.

**Entrepreneur (ESTP) — Se-Ti-Fe-Ni**
Leads with what's tangible right now (Se). Logic is internal and quick (Ti). Inferior Ni means they don't naturally hold a long-term vision — they trust what they can see working today and adjust in real time.
**How they decide:** show me, then I'll trust the framework.

**Executive (ESTJ) — Te-Si-Ne-Fi**
Leads with metrics (Te) anchored in proven process (Si). Tertiary Ne means they will entertain new approaches but only after the proven baseline is established. Inferior Fi means they hold their values privately and won't voice them unless pushed.
**How they decide:** prove the system works, then I can hold you to it.

### Analytical / Deliberate

**Architect (INTJ) — Ni-Te-Fi-Se**
Leads with long-range vision (Ni). Te executes that vision through metrics and systems. Inferior Se means they're slow to act on what's right in front of them — they often miss tactical opportunities while pursuing the strategic one.
**How they decide:** does this serve the long-term vision and is the logic airtight?

**Logician (INTP) — Ti-Ne-Si-Fe**
Leads with internal logic (Ti) tested across many possibilities (Ne). Will probe your assumptions to find the inconsistency. Inferior Fe means they are weak at reading room dynamics — they may push a logical point past the social cost of pushing it.
**How they decide:** is the framework internally consistent? Have I tested every angle?

**Logistician (ISTJ) — Si-Te-Fi-Ne**
Leads with proven process and historical data (Si) executed through metrics (Te). Inferior Ne means they're slow to embrace novel approaches — they need precedent before they trust the new thing.
**How they decide:** show me how this has worked before, with the same kind of company I am.

**Virtuoso (ISTP) — Ti-Se-Ni-Fe**
Leads with internal logic (Ti) applied to what's right in front of them (Se). Sees how the mechanism works. Inferior Fe means low patience for relationship-building when the substance isn't there yet.
**How they decide:** is the mechanism real and is it tangible right now?

### Relationship-Led

**Advocate (INFJ) — Ni-Fe-Ti-Se**
Leads with vision (Ni) calibrated to others' wellbeing (Fe). Tertiary Ti gives them analytical rigor when they choose to use it. Inferior Se means they live in the long-term — present-moment tactics feel hollow.
**How they decide:** does this serve a meaningful long-term outcome and is the human cost right?

**Protagonist (ENFJ) — Fe-Ni-Se-Ti**
Leads with group harmony and others' needs (Fe). Vision underneath (Ni). Tertiary Se shows up as warmth in the present moment. Inferior Ti means they are slower to challenge the logical foundation of an idea — they trust the room over the framework.
**How they decide:** is everyone aligned and is this serving the larger purpose?

**Defender (ISFJ) — Si-Fe-Ti-Ne**
Leads with proven process (Si) calibrated to others' comfort (Fe). Inferior Ne means they distrust novel approaches and need precedent — especially socially proven precedent (people they trust having used you).
**How they decide:** has this worked before for someone like us, and is everyone comfortable?

**Consul (ESFJ) — Fe-Si-Ne-Ti**
Leads with the group (Fe), anchored in what's worked before (Si). Inferior Ti means they make decisions through social and historical alignment rather than logical analysis — they may struggle to defend a decision purely on logic.
**How they decide:** is the team aligned and does this match how we've done things?

### Vision / Energy

**Campaigner (ENFP) — Ne-Fi-Te-Si**
Leads with possibilities (Ne) filtered through personal values (Fi). Tertiary Te gives them execution capacity when activated. Inferior Si means they get fatigued by detail and procedure quickly.
**How they decide:** does this excite me and align with what I believe?

**Debater (ENTP) — Ne-Ti-Fe-Si**
Leads with possibilities (Ne) tested through internal logic (Ti). Loves the debate. Inferior Si means they get bored with proven process — they'd rather invent the new approach than execute the proven one.
**How they decide:** is the idea innovative and does the logic hold under pressure?

### Mission / Autonomy

**Mediator (INFP) — Fi-Ne-Si-Te**
Leads with private values (Fi) layered onto possibilities (Ne). Inferior Te means they distrust pure metrics and aggressive sales motion — they shut down under pressure.
**How they decide:** does this align with what I believe and does it feel authentic?

**Adventurer (ISFP) — Fi-Se-Ni-Te**
Leads with personal values (Fi) and what's tangible right now (Se). Inferior Te means they reject metric-heavy pitches and aggressive close motion.
**How they decide:** does this feel right and is it real and immediate?

**Entertainer (ESFP) — Se-Fi-Te-Ni**
Leads with the present moment and energy (Se), filtered through values (Fi). Inferior Ni means they are weak on long-term planning — focus stays present.
**How they decide:** does the energy feel right and do I trust this person right now?

## How to Use This in Diagnosis

When typing a buyer from a transcript, listen for the **dominant function** first — that's the loudest signal:

- "What's the ROI?" / "What's the number?" → **Te** dominant (Commander, Executive)
- "Walk me through the framework / underlying logic" → **Ti** dominant (Logician, Virtuoso)
- "How does the team feel?" / "Is everyone aligned?" → **Fe** dominant (Protagonist, Consul)
- "Does this match what we believe?" / long pause before answering → **Fi** dominant (Mediator, Adventurer)
- "What if we...?" / rapid idea-generation → **Ne** dominant (Campaigner, Debater)
- "Where is this heading long-term?" / strong vision language → **Ni** dominant (Advocate, Architect)
- "Show me, right now" / hands-on focus → **Se** dominant (Entrepreneur, Entertainer)
- "How has this worked before?" / specific past-precedent questions → **Si** dominant (Logistician, Defender)

Then listen for the **auxiliary** to narrow which of the two types it is:

- Te-dominant + Ni auxiliary = Commander
- Te-dominant + Si auxiliary = Executive

The **inferior function** often surfaces as the buyer's blind spot in the deal — the thing they will under-weight or get wrong:

- Inferior Fi (Commander, Executive) → may override values for ROI; values pitch lands late but powerfully
- Inferior Se (Architect, Advocate) → may miss tactical opportunities while pursuing the strategic one
- Inferior Ne (Logistician, Defender) → may reject novel approaches even when proven elsewhere
- Inferior Ti (Protagonist, Consul) → may struggle to defend a decision purely on logic
- Inferior Si (Campaigner, Debater) → may resist proven process and want to invent the new way
- Inferior Te (Mediator, Adventurer) → shuts down under metrics and pressure
- Inferior Ni (Entrepreneur, Entertainer) → weak on long-term planning, focus stays present
- Inferior Fe (Logician, Virtuoso) → may push past social cost when making a logical point

Use the inferior to predict where the seller has leverage *and* where the deal is most likely to break.
